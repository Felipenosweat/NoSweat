The studio

Dani Okafor runs Fade Theory, a barber studio in East Austin that does skin fades, tapers, textured crops, beard sculpting and hair designs. Appointments only.

Services

Skin fades to hair designs

  • Skin fade

    Cut down to skin at the bottom and blended up into the length on top.

  • Taper

    Shorter at the neckline and around the ears, with the length kept on top.

  • Textured crop

    A short cut with texture through the top and a clean fringe.

  • Beard sculpting

    Shape, line and blend the beard into the fade.

  • Hair designs

    Lines and shapes cut into the hair.

The music is loud and the room is clean.
